Immortal Chase

A chibi xianxia race: catch the Star-Jade Hare, flee the Giant Jade Spider.

To install it: open the browser's menu ⋮ and tap Install app (or Add to Home screen). On an iPhone: the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.

What “install” means

The game is added to your home screen with its own icon, like any app, without the Play Store. It opens full screen and sideways, and starts even without internet (against the bots).

It updates itself: every time you open it online, it is the latest version. Nothing to download again.

Everything runs on your phone. Races with friends go phone to phone; the site only delivers the game. To remove it, long-press its icon and choose Uninstall.

Racing with friends

One of you taps Host a race and shares the room (the Share button, or the QR code). The others tap the link, scan the QR code, or tap Join a race and type the code.
