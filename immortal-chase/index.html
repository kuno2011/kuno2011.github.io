Immortal Chase

A chibi xianxia race for Android: catch the Star-Jade Hare, flee the Giant Jade Spider.

▶ Play now in your browser

Install it from there to play it like an app: in Chrome, the menu ⋮ then Install app (or Add to Home screen). It updates itself every time you open it. Turn your phone sideways to play.

⬇ Or download the Android app (APK)

Installing the Android app

  1. Tap the download button above, then open the file once it has downloaded.
  2. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps (it is not from the Play Store).
  3. Tap Install. To update later, download it again and install over the old one: your settings stay.

Racing with friends

One of you taps Host a race and shares the room (the Share button, or the QR code). The others tap the link, scan the QR code, or tap Join a race and type the code.
